THE FILES

Take the whole pack, or just one sheet.

Every document is version 1.1.0, dated 22 September 2026. PDFs are for printing; Word documents are editable. Print A4 at actual size. Dividers are six separate pages. For the medicines sheet, print both sides on one sheet, flipping on the long edge; its reverse has three spaces for spare pharmacy labels or copies. Keep original labels on the medicine packaging.
