A note about the pack

For your own notes and understanding. It does not replace your clinical records or professional advice. This first release has not been clinically validated.

Start where you are

You don’t have to
catch up first.

You can begin at your next appointment. The older letters can wait.

Read the one-page quick start ↗
  1. 01

    Put today at the front.

    Use any A4 folder. Add the current information you know and a dated medicines list. Write “not sure” for anything that needs checking.

  2. 02

    Take one sheet to the next visit.

    Choose your questions beforehand. Make a note of what you understand, what happens next, who arranges it and by when.

  3. 03

    Add the past as you go.

    File older letters when you have time. Keep originals intact and date your summaries. Use only the sections that help you.
